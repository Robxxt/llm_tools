CV Job Form Filler

Local backend settings — provider, CV and connection.

1 Model provider

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (Ollama, OpenRouter, OpenAI…). Stored locally in settings.json.

Never written to settings.json. For a persistent key add CVFILL_API_KEY=... (or OPENROUTER_API_KEY) to backend/.env and restart the backend; typing one here overrides it for this session.

2 Your CV

Paste your CV as plain text or parse a PDF. The extension reads this from the backend.

Nothing leaves your machine except calls to the provider above. The extension opens the backend at 127.0.0.1:5000.
